About This Property

In Xanadu did Kubla Khan a stately pleasure-dome decree — and on the northern slope of South Mountain, another pleasure-dome rose from desert stone, as if conjured by the same fierce dreaming. Here, where the demon woman wailed for her demon lover beneath a waning moon, where Saguaros stand sentinel and arroyos carve the earth on three sides like a moat of God's own making — here stands the Urban Farmhouse. Dark skies absolute. No future building permitted. The mountain at your back, eternity ahead. 4.4 acres on the largest City Park in America. 5,400 square feet of deliberate living — Barn, Pool House, 8 levels of landscape architecture ascending like stanzas. The largest saltwater pool in Maricopa County, 85,000 gallons, a mirror for the Milky Way.

75 minutes from Los Angeles airports, yet so removed from the world's fever that Haboobs roll toward you like revelation and the city lights below seem someone else's dream. For the Creative who needs space to think. The CEO who needs space to breathe. The investor who understands that land this rare, this protected, this impossible — is never built again. Come, possess it. As Donne wrote — one little room, an everywhere.
