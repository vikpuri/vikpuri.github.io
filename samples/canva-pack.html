How to publish to Canva

  1. Take a screenshot of each asset above (full quality, single asset per shot).
  2. Open Canva → Create a design → choose the matching size:
    • 8.5 × 11" for the QR Flash Poster
    • 1080 × 1080 for the Instagram Square
    • 5 × 7" for the Countertop Card
    • 1920 × 1080 for Digital Signage
  3. Drop the screenshot in as a base layer; overlay your editable text + QR using Canva's tools.
  4. Save as a Canva template; submit to Canva's template marketplace as "Designed for Requation."
  5. Each Canva template links back to requation.com via Canva's brand attribution. Their 100M+ users see our brand on every download.
